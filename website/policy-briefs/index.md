# Policy Briefs

Mainstay policy briefs connect practical local-first infrastructure with the
enduring responsibilities of communities and institutions. They are written
for leaders, operators, policy makers, and partners who need to understand the
purpose and implications of the work without beginning with its protocols or
deployment details.

## Clerk and Treasury Functions

Every institutionally sustainable community needs ways to preserve records and
move value. These can be understood as **Clerk** and **Treasury** functions.
The names are functional shorthand, not prescribed offices or governance
models.

Mainstay is not inventing these responsibilities. It is exploring how to
reconstitute age-old institutional capabilities with local-first software,
portable cryptographic identities, verifiable records, private bearer
instruments, and infrastructure that can continue operating when outside
services are unavailable.
